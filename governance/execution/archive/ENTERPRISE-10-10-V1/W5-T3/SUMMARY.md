product-validatable capability catalog for the agent registry.

* capability schema

* dependency model with `requires`, `supports` and `incompatibleWith`

* local W5-T3 validator
* roadmap coverage and roadmap-to-files updates that keep W5-T4+ open

W5-T4 remains open and is the next eligible task.
